How we solve it

Research every prospect.

Mirenta pulls the public story of each founder and company and builds a one-pager that mirrors their world back to them. You see your own priorities on the first page. That recognition opens the conversation and earns replies in a way generic templates do not.

Write founder to founder.

The one-pager carries the voice of a peer who hit the same wall. It reads like advice from someone in the trenches, not a vendor pitch. Every concrete detail (the 19-day bank wait, the design-partner loop, the weekly build-in-public updates) lands because it proves we have watched founders and know what matters to you.

Free your hours.

You spend 10 seconds instead of an hour on customization. The engine researches and builds while you sleep. You still own the voice and the decision; Mirenta handles the research lift. That shift alone lets you run outreach at scale without losing the personalization that makes it work.
